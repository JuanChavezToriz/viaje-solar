# Textura galáctica artística

Herramienta: ImageGen integrada. Ilustración artística para un disco 3D; no es una fotografía de la Vía Láctea.

## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: a square face-on galaxy disk texture for an interactive 3D astronomy website.
Primary request: create a convincing astrophotography-inspired artistic view of the Milky Way as a barred spiral galaxy, entirely viewed straight down its rotation axis in an orthographic top-down view. This is a luminous galactic disk, NOT an illustration of a whirlpool.
Composition: complete circular galactic disk centered in the image, occupying about 82% of the square, with comfortable transparent margin all around. Exact face-on orientation, no tilt or perspective, no cropped arms.
Structure: a substantial softly glowing warm cream and amber central bulge with a short elongated central bar, fading gradually into a continuous diffuse disk of countless old stars. Two irregular dominant spiral arm complexes plus fragmented minor branches merge into that disk. Natural asymmetry, broad feathered stellar clouds, coherent dusty structure and subtle mottled detail. Deep dark brown branching dust absorption lanes thread through the luminous disk and arms. Outer stellar clouds have muted cool blue-grey and pale ivory tones with a few tiny subdued rosy star-forming knots. Use realistic hierarchical detail: broad diffuse light, fine grain, and only a few distinguishable bright stars.
Exposure: restrained cinematic astrophotographic exposure with detail retained in the nucleus and all arms. The center is luminous but not clipped. The arms are softer and dimmer than the center. No pure white bright ribbons, no repeated evenly spaced four-arm pinwheel, no neon, no geometric smooth lines, no huge glowing star points, no bright purple color cast.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha outside the galactic disk. The faint outer halo fades smoothly to transparency. Keep dust-lane detail within the galaxy.
Constraints: no text, labels, logo, frame, constellation lines, planets, lens flare, foreground stars or stars outside the galaxy. This must work as a high-quality reusable texture on a 3D disk.
